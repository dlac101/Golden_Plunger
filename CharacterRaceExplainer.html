The Character Race

Runs like The Amazing Race: all speed and strategy, no luck. Here is how it works, and why every team still has room to move.

How the race works

Run the circuit

1
Five locationsEach supersquad runs a circuit of 5 different locations around camp.
2
Pick one of two tasksAt every location there are two tasks. Complete one to earn that location's marker.
3
Beat the trafficOnly one team can work a task at a time, so plan your order to avoid waiting in line.
4
First to all 5 markers wins the placeThe supersquad that finishes its full circuit soonest takes the top placement.
5
Strategy, not chanceNo scavenger hunt, no luck. It is a planned task race, start to finish.

All 12 supersquads (4 teams × 3 squads) are ranked in finish order, 1st all the way down to 12th.
