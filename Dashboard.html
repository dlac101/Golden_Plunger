How We Score Your Cabin

Every day, an inspector checks 5 things in your cabin:

0 Needs work
1 Getting there
2 Awesome job!

Add up all 5 scores, and that’s your cabin’s Cleanliness score, out of 10 points!

Sparkle Points & Winning the Day

Sparkle is a bonus score that starts at 5. If your cabin really shines, it can go up. If it’s a rough day, it can go down a little.

Sparkle almost never matters on its own. It’s only there to break ties!

Each day, the cabin with the highest Cleanliness score wins for its age group. Exactly tied on Cleanliness? Sparkle breaks it. Still tied? The inspector decides by hand.
