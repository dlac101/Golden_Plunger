MATTHEW 7:24–27 Basketball Court

Building on the Rock

  • Pick one counselor to stand inside the box.
  • Relay cups one at a time and stack them on the counselor.
  • The counselor must stay completely still the whole time.
  • Each cup is stacked on its own, never nested inside another.
  • No holding cups in place with your hands.
  • Build the tallest tower you can before time runs out.
  • If it topples, rebuild and keep going.
5 points per cup stacked when time is called
MATTHEW 7:13–14 Canteen Porch

The Narrow Gate

  • Everyone grips the outer edge of the tarp at all times.
  • Tilt together to steer each sphere across to the far side.
  • Keep the spheres out of the holes cut in the tarp.
  • Guide each sphere through the narrow gate into the bucket.
10 points per sphere landed in the bucket
GALATIANS 6:2 Volleyball Court (by low blocks)

Bear One Another's Burdens

  • Every squad member holds at least one rope on the tube.
  • Work together to roll the sphere into the far bucket, keeping it on the tube.
  • Stay outside the course: steer only by pulling the ropes.
  • If the sphere hits the ground, the whole team restarts.
10 points per sphere in the bucket
2 CORINTHIANS 5:7 Open Area (LVL to the Lake)

Faith Over Sight

  • Squads split into callers and seekers, swapping roles as they go, max 3 seekers in the field at once.
  • Seekers are blindfolded and must locate flags scattered across the field.
  • Callers stay outside the boundary and steer their seeker using voice only.
  • Listen closely, find a flag, and carry it safely back.
  • Return a flag, then tag in the next teammate.
5 points per flag before time is called
ROMANS 15:13 The Lake

Overflowing!

  • One player at a time fills a cup with water.
  • Before the run, catapult the cup skyward and catch it, no spilling.
  • Relay the caught cup to your squad's tube and pour it in.
  • Rising water lifts the spheres until they float out the top.
  • No hands on the spheres, water does the work.
5 points per sphere that floats out of the tube
PHILIPPIANS 3:14 Dining Hall Porch

Upward Call

  • Every player gets one pair of chopsticks.
  • Work together to lift spheres up and out of the pit, chopsticks only.
  • No hands on the spheres or the pit.
  • Once a sphere clears the top, guide it into the team bucket.
  • Keep going until every sphere is moved or time runs out.
10 points per sphere transferred to the bucket
The Finale

Crack the Cipher

  • Each finished challenge hands your squad one cipher piece.
  • Your final challenge hands over a coded piece of Scripture.
  • Head to the lakefront and use your cipher pieces to decode it.
  • Squads on the same color team arrange the pieces in order.
  • Read the full Scripture aloud, and when it's right, your team's timer stops.
